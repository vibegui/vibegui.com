# Outline: Honey, I Shrunk the Agency!

**Slug**: honey-i-shrunk-the-agency
**Structure**: Long-form (Section 4.2) blended with Technical Build (4.3) for the rooms/factory section
**Emotional Arc**: Pattern B — Provocation → Reframe → Declaration
**Target Length**: 1200–1600 words

## Beats

### Beat 1: Hook — Manifesto in one breath
- **What happens**: Open with the punchline; immediately stake that this is about shrinking the agency, not chatbots.
- **Key points**: Honey line; pocket + browser; self-evolving storefront as the thing being built.
- **Emotional register**: Bold, slightly playful, confident.
- **Research to use**: none yet — pure voice.
- **~Words**: 80–120

### Beat 2: Not the shopping agent story
- **What happens**: Draw the line against buyer-side agentic commerce hype. Merchant-side factory is the game.
- **Key points**: Forrester tempering; press chases checkout agents; we care about who tends the store overnight.
- **Emotional register**: Clearing fog — sharp, slightly impatient.
- **Research to use**: Forrester mid-2026; eMarketer agentic volume as background (optional, light).
- **~Words**: 120–160

### Beat 3: Honest assessment — what an agency already is
- **What happens**: Name the real operating model agencies run: AM + Kanban + specialists + ship + measure. Admit we've been that agency for years; the cost is the problem.
- **Key points**: Retainer bands ($10k–$50k full-service); most founders can't buy the full bench; Sequoia $1:$6; sell the work not the tool.
- **Emotional register**: Grounded, arena — we've lived this.
- **Research to use**: Agency pricing 2026; Sequoia services thesis.
- **~Words**: 220–280

### Beat 4: The reframe — self-evolving = software factory
- **What happens**: Define the loop with brutal concreteness: inputs → metrics → decisions → actions (PRs, deploys) → observation → learning → inputs.
- **Key points**: ASCII or tight prose loop; overnight diagnose→fix→optimize; metrics as scoreboard (CWV, conversion, errors, SEO, GMV).
- **Emotional register**: Clarity after fog — "aha."
- **Research to use**: Internal factory language; M2 overnight loop.
- **~Words**: 180–220

### Beat 5: Compression map — rooms, threads, agents, sandboxes
- **What happens**: The technical heart. Map agency roles onto software primitives. Mention Buzz as cousin, not dependency.
- **Key points**:
  - Room = repo
  - Thread = branch (PR + CI + review + preview in one stream)
  - Agent = role (AM, coding, QA — not one super-agent)
  - Sandbox = desk / work session
  - Kanban = shared state
  - Buzz: branch-as-channel, humans+agents same room (Jul 2026)
- **Emotional register**: Excited builder explaining over coffee.
- **Research to use**: Autofix daily architecture; Buzz launch + forge vision.
- **~Words**: 320–400

### Beat 6: The stack we're building (in public)
- **What happens**: Name deco pieces without brochure voice — diagnostics front door, Kanban prioritization, autofix sandbox→PR→QA, observe again. Admit unfinished.
- **Key points**: Free diagnostic / limited autofix; gold-silver-zinc findings; QA gates as trust; GitHub still the actuator.
- **Emotional register**: Vulnerable honesty — we're in the arena shipping this.
- **Research to use**: Internal PLG/autofix; production AI failure rates as why gates matter.
- **~Words**: 200–260

### Beat 7: Forward look — every entrepreneur gets one
- **What happens**: Who this unlocks. Brazil / underdog optional light touch. Commitment: agency outcomes without agency headcount.
- **Key points**: Pocket + browser reprise once; judgment stays at prioritization; execution compresses.
- **Emotional register**: Invitation without slogans.
- **Research to use**: Sequoia wedge (outsourced intelligence-heavy first).
- **~Words**: 120–160

### Beat 8: Close
- **What happens**: Grounded statement or quiet invitation — not engagement bait.
- **Key points**: One line that lands the punchline without repeating it thrice.
- **Emotional register**: Calm certainty.
- **~Words**: 40–60

- Vulnerable moment: years of running agency-shaped delivery for brands; watching retainers gatekeep who gets a real digital operation.

- English, long-form manifesto energy, first person throughout.
