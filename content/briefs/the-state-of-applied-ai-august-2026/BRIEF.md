## The one point

> The race stopped being about which model you use. It is about who builds the factory around it.

Stated in the first sentence. Everything else in the piece exists to support it.

## Angle

The last three years look like fashion and are not. Each new term — agentic workflows, vibe coding, context engineering, harness, context graphs — was somebody discovering another part of the same machine, before anyone had drawn the machine. The software factory is the zoom-out that puts all of them in one picture: goal and constraints, inputs that are signals rather than tickets, processes and memory and workers inside, outputs that are actions in the world, and a loop back from what those actions did to the numbers.

Setting one up is the new race. We are building that for digital experience at decocms.com.

## Target Audience

Ecommerce and technology executives. They know PDP, GMV, Core Web Vitals. They have run a pilot that impressed nobody. They are being sold model comparisons and want to know what actually moves money.

## Key Message

Model choice has a shelf life of about six weeks and everyone will make the same one. The durable asset is the loop you build around it: something that watches your operation, decides what is worth building, ships it, proves it on live traffic, and remembers the answer.
