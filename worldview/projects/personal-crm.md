**Spirit:** nothing is more important than keeping up with important connections.

## Declared outcome

A concentric-circle map of relationships where proximity is driven by contact
frequency, not a static list. Ingests real WhatsApp conversations and LinkedIn
network data, then surfaces who to reply to, which relationships have gone
dormant, and where an opportunity exists.

## Success criteria

Working end to end: export WhatsApp chats and LinkedIn network → parsed and scored
by recency and frequency → concentric-circle map renders → at least one real
surfaced action validated against actual relationships.

Active, 2 open goals. A local WhatsApp scraper exists but was never wired into the
PRM. The weekend goal for 2026-08-01/02 — revive the scraper and land the first
real update — has passed unacknowledged.
