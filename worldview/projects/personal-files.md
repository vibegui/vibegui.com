**Spirit:** my digital world should be findable and intentional.

## Declared outcome

Files across Mac, iCloud, and Google Drive searchable as one private map, with a
knowable canonical copy and reversible operations only. This is integrity of
objects at the personal-data layer.

## Success criteria

1. All three sources indexed and visible in one map.
2. Canonical and duplicate candidates distinguishable.
3. Every move or archive has a dry run, an audit trail, and a recovery path — zero
   unrecoverable automated operations.

## Where it actually is

Draft, not started. One open capture since 2026-07-18: sketch the read-only local
index schema.

## Archived — superseded by `zelador`

zelador is this outcome as a real app: read-only scan, heuristics first, every move approved, full recovery path.
