**Spirit:** the advice I wanted when starting my company.

## Declared outcome

Turn company diagnosis into an iterative founder journey. A Brazilian founder
arrives with a real situation, gets a diagnosis they recognise as true, and leaves
with the next move — the conversation I would have wanted at 25 and did not have
access to.

This is `understanding` pointed at one specific audience: the distinctions a
founder needs, delivered through a conversation that lands rather than an essay
they bounce off.

## Success criteria

1. Founders can diagnose, improve, package, and share their company.
2. At least one founder reports a genuine change in perspective — not "useful
   tool", a distinction they got.
3. The diagnosis questionnaire produces a reading the founder recognises as
   accurate before it offers advice.
